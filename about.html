    About Shefa General Hospital

    Started as a 30-bed clinic in 2004, today we serve over 1,200 outpatients every day.

    Who we are

    Shefa General Hospital was founded by a group of local doctors who wanted families in the district to get specialist care without travelling to Dhaka.

    We run a full emergency department, a 20-bed ICU, a labour ward and a diagnostic centre under one roof. Our 60+ consultants are supported by 300 nurses and technicians.

    We follow national treatment guidelines and keep our prices displayed at the front desk, so patients know the cost before they pay.

    Our journey

    1. 2004Opened as a 30-bed clinic with 5 doctors.
    2. 2011New building with 150 beds and an operation theatre complex.
    3. 2016ICU, CCU and NICU opened.
    4. 2022CT scan and 24-hour pathology added.
    5. 2025Expanded to 250 beds.
